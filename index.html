01 about

I help build products from the very first line of code.

I'm a full-stack software engineer with 5+ years of experience building, shipping and supporting production web applications. Most of my work is in Ruby on Rails, together with Hotwire, Stimulus, JavaScript and TypeScript on the frontend.

At benefitbay, I joined as one of two engineers on the original four-person team and worked closely with the VP of Engineering to build our insurance technology platform from scratch. Being on a small team meant I worked on the whole process: planning and designing features, then building, testing, deploying and supporting them in production.

Before engineering, I worked in project management in international teams, and that still shapes how I work: clear communication, practical priorities and caring about the people who use what we build.
